How to play

Bugs land on a clean white screen. Tap them before they escape — every escape costs a life. Never tap the ladybug: it's harmless, and touching it costs a life too. Golden beetles are rare and worth double. Every 65 swats the Queen arrives; hit her again and again while you clear the larvae she lays.

FAQ

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free. You only see one if you choose to: when you lose your last life, "Continue" lets you watch a short video ad and keep your run going with one life and a clean screen. You can continue once per run. Otherwise the game has no ads.

I watched an ad but didn't get to continue.

The reward is only given if the ad plays to the end. If you closed it early, the Continue button returns as soon as the next ad is ready.

Why don't I see the Continue button?

It only appears when an ad has finished loading, which needs an internet connection. It also doesn't appear a second time in the same run.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking can be changed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, you can also review your ad choices with "Privacy" on the start screen. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why is there no sound?

Check "Sound" on the start or pause screen. The game also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

What if I get a call or leave the app mid-run?

The game pauses automatically and continues with a 3-2-1 countdown when you come back.

How do I reset my progress?

Your best score, streak and collection are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later.
