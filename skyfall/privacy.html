Free Fall — Privacy Policy

Developer: Muhammet Bozkurt · Effective 27 September 2026

Summary: Free Fall doesn't have accounts and we don't run our own servers. The game shows optional video ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to serve them, as described in section 3.

1. Data stored on your device

  • The app saves your best depth and your sound on/off setting locally on your device.
  • This data is never sent to us. It may be included in your own device or iCloud backups.
  • Deleting the app removes it.

2. Game Center

  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard and shown with your Game Center nickname.
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center data.
  • You can stop this by signing out of Game Center in the Settings app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • After a crash you can choose to watch a video ad to continue your run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.
  • To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects data from your device, including:
    • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
    • device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
    • ad interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped)
    • diagnostic and crash data from the ads SDK
    • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • This data goes to Google, not to us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: Free Fall asks once whether it may track you. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, but not personalised ones.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: you're asked for consent before any ads load, and you can change your choice with "Privacy settings" on the start and game-over screens.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for advertising with "Privacy settings" on the start and game-over screens.
  • Watching an ad is always optional; the game is fully playable without it.

5. What we don't do

We have no accounts, no analytics of our own, no servers, and we never sell your data. The only third-party SDK is Google's Mobile Ads SDK (with Google's consent SDK).

6. Children

Free Fall is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
