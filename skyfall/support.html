How to play

Walls rise toward your skydiver, each with a hole shaped like a pose. Tap the matching pose at the bottom of the screen before the wall arrives. One wrong pose ends the run. Your score is how deep you fall.

FAQ

How do I unlock Frog and Runner?

They unlock during a run: Frog at wall 12, Runner at wall 28. Every run starts again with the first four poses.

What are the walls with a "?" on them?

Memory walls, which appear from wall 20 onward. The hole fades out before it reaches you, so remember its shape.

Why is there no sound?

Check the speaker button at the top right of the screen. The game also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

Where is the leaderboard?

The Leaderboard button appears on the start and game-over screens when you're signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center).

What if I get a call or leave the app mid-run?

The game pauses automatically and continues when you come back.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Free Fall free. You only see one if you choose to: after a crash, "Watch ad to continue" lets you keep your run going. Otherwise the game has no ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

To change whether Free Fall may track you, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, you can also tap "Privacy settings" on the start or game-over screen. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I reset my best score?

Your best score is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it. Game Center leaderboard scores are kept by Apple.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
